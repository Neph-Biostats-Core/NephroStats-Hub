Our study **["Dialysis Facility Closures in the US From 2018 to 2024: A Serial Cross-Sectional Study"](https://www.ajkd.org/article/S0272-6386(26)00754-7/fulltext)**, published in the June 2026 issue of the *American Journal of Kidney Diseases* (AJKD), has been selected as one of seven **[Editors' Choice](https://www.ajkd.org/content/edchoice)** articles of 2026.

The editors also chose the article to receive the **inaugural James S. Kaufman Early Career Award**, named in honor of [Dr. James S. Kaufman](https://ajkdblog.org/2025/09/04/remembering-and-honoring-james-kaufman-md/).

AJKD received more than 1,200 Original Investigation submissions over the past year and will publish 75 in 2026. The study, led by Meri R. J. Varkila with Core members Maria Emilia Montez Rath and Snow Yu, examines dialysis facility openings and closures across the US and finds that closures outpaced openings between late 2021 and early 2024, with closures more common in rural areas and the Midwest.

AJKD will announce the award on the [AJKD Blog](https://ajkdblog.org/tag/editors-choice/) during Kidney Week, and the article will be freely available for 90 days.

Congratulations to all co-authors!
