The **Biostatistics Core** provides and facilitates statistical collaborations involving the design, conduct, analysis and publication of kidney-related research conducted by faculty and fellows of the Division of Nephrology.

#### Project & grant support

Planning a study, grant or manuscript? Submit a request and we'll match you with a statistician.

- [Project/Grant Support Request Form]({{ L.support_request_form }})
- [Protocol Template]({{ L.protocol_template }})

#### Office hours

We hold office hours **twice monthly** for quick questions. Two statisticians, one Master's and one Doctorate level, are available at each session. [Sign up here]({{ L.office_hours_signup }}) to receive a Zoom link.

#### Kidney Clinical Research (KCR) conference

Mondays: didactic lectures and research presentations. It's an opportunity to discuss methods, grant applications, practice presentations and more.
[Full schedule]({{ L.kcr_schedule }}) · [Sign up to present]({{ L.kcr_signup }})
